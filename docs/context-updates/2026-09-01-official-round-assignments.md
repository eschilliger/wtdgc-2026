# 2026-09-01 - Official round assignments

The Participant Guide now provides the official Singles/Doubles pattern for Open and Masters rounds 1-8.

## France roster rules used by the app

- Open requires exactly 4 men as MPO and 2 women as FPO. An FPO player cannot occupy an MPO slot in the France workflow.
- Masters requires exactly 3 men as MP40, Mehdi Boukarabila (PDGA 69452) as MP50, and 2 women as FP40.
- Within MPO, FPO, MP40 and FP40, positions are assigned from the event reference rating in descending order.

## Round workflow

- Every round contains exactly four fixed Games: Simple 1, Simple 2, Double 1 and Double 2.
- France players are generated automatically from the selected six, their ITPR positions and the official round table.
- Staff manually selects the six opponent players in the four Games.
- A player cannot be assigned to more than one opponent Game.
- Ready/published status requires a valid France six and six distinct opponent players. Published status still requires a scheduled start.
- The saved round now stores a stable `game` key and Masters slot assignments as well as the selected player ids.

## Player area

`Mes matchs` displays the official Game label and continues to expose only assignments involving the linked player.
